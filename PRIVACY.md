# Privacy Policy for Clipzy AI

**Effective Date:** May 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Clipzy AI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how Clipzy AI collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect
Clipzy AI only requests and collects data necessary to perform the application's core functionality:
- **Google & YouTube Account Data**: When you connect your Google Account, we request access to your email address, public profile information, and permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload`).
- **Video & Audio Content**: Video files or URLs you upload or import to create short-form AI video clips.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
- To authenticate your Google / YouTube account via Google OAuth 2.0.
- To upload and publish AI-edited video clips directly to your personal YouTube channel upon your explicit command.
- To process speech-to-text transcription and video curation.

## 4. Google API Limited Use Disclosure
Clipzy AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
- We **DO NOT** sell, rent, or trade your Google or YouTube account data to any third parties or advertisers.
- We **DO NOT** use your Google user data for advertising purposes or AI model training.

## 5. Data Storage and Security
- Your Google OAuth access tokens are stored securely on your device's internal storage.
- Video files processed in the cloud are cached temporarily to complete rendering and are automatically deleted.

## 6. User Control and Account Disconnection
You can revoke Clipzy AI's access to your Google Account at any time by:
- Tapping "Switch / Disconnect Account" inside the Clipzy AI app.
- Visiting your [Google Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and removing access for Clipzy AI.

## 7. Third-Party Services
Clipzy AI utilizes the following third-party services:
- **Google Play Services / YouTube Data API v3**: For video publishing and account authentication.
- **Cloudflare R2**: For temporary video processing storage.
